1. Our principle

CarReviews exists so that riders can share honest experiences. We want genuine reviews, both positive and negative, to stay up. We act on content only according to the written rules below.

2. Reasons we hide or remove content

ReasonExamples
SpamAdvertising, promotion links, repeated or automated submissions, content unrelated to the company.
AbuseThreats, hate speech, harassment, obscene language aimed at a person, encouragement of violence.
FakeMade-up experiences, copied reviews, several reviews from one person through multiple accounts or numbers.
Conflict of interestReviews by owners, employees, drivers, their relatives, competitors, or anyone paid or rewarded for the review.
PrivacyHome addresses, personal phone numbers, license or plate numbers linked to a person, health or financial details, or identifying a private individual unnecessarily.
LegalA court order, content that is clearly unlawful, or a valid copyright or trademark notice.
OtherAnother clear violation of our Terms of Use. The specific rule is written down in our records.

Where possible, we hide content instead of deleting it, so that a decision can be reviewed and reversed.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not hide or remove a review because it is positive or negative, or because of its rating.
  • We do not accept payment, discounts or any benefit to remove, hide, change, add or rank reviews.
  • We do not edit the wording of a user's review. We may hide a review, or (with the writer's agreement) publish a version with private information removed.
  • We do not tell a company who wrote a review, unless the law requires it.

4. Voice messages

  • Our administrator types the written version of a voice message word for word, as it was said. Before publishing, the administrator confirms this, and every change to the written text is recorded.
  • A voice message that breaks these rules is not played and not published.

5. Reporting a review

  • Anyone can report a review with the "Report" button next to it, or on the Report page. No account is needed. You must give an e-mail address so we can answer you.
  • To prevent abuse, one person can send up to 5 reports per hour.
  • We confirm receipt within , and we aim to decide within . Complicated cases may take longer, and we will tell you.
  • False or bad-faith reports may be ignored.

6. Company requests

  • A company owner or a person authorized to act for the company can use the Report page to: report a review, correct company details (phone, name, areas), ask to respond to reviews, or ask for removal.
  • We may ask for proof that you act for the company.
  • Being listed is free, and a company cannot pay to remove or change reviews.

7. How we decide

  • We read the report, the review, and any evidence. We may ask the reporter or the reviewer for more information or evidence (for example, trip records, without sharing who the reviewer is).
  • We decide whether to keep, hide or remove the content, and we write down the reason (from the list in section 2).
  • If we cannot confirm that a review breaks a rule, we generally keep it.
  • Hidden content is not shown to anyone, including the writer.

8. Asking us to look again

If you think we made a mistake, either because we kept a review or because we hid or removed one, write to with the details. Someone will look at the case again, and we will tell you the result.

9. Repeat violations

Users who repeatedly break these rules may lose permission to write, and their account or phone number may be blocked.

10. Records

We keep a record of every hide, remove and keep decision, the reason, and the date, so that decisions are consistent and can be checked.
